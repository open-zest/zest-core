1. The Carrier Objection ("The Naive Zone-Stowage Fallacy")

When rail and terminal operators propose pre-sorting containers by inland destination at the Port of Origin (PoO) so deep-sea vessels can discharge in pure block-train batches at European seaports, ocean carrier stowage teams rightfully object to naive geographic grouping:

  1. Hydrostatic Stability & Trim (GM, LCG, TCG): Grouping all containers bound for a single inland zone (e.g., ZONE_A_DUI Duisburg) into one bay without mass stratification violates Metacentric Height (GM ≥ 1.50 m), Longitudinal Center of Gravity (LCG), or Transverse list (TCG) limits.
  2. Hull Girder Shear Force (SF) & Bending Moments (BM): Concentrating dense cargo in contiguous bays exceeds Classification Society (DNV / Lloyd's Register) permissible still-water bending moments.
  3. Origin Yard Re-Handles ("Shifters"): Digging through origin yard stacks to retrieve a specific container serial number (BIC) for a vessel slot destroys origin loading crane productivity.

Project ZeST resolves all three constraints by extending the standard Container Ship Stowage Planning Problem (CSPP) from a 3-Tensor Master Bay Plan to a 4-Tensor Weight-Zone Equivalence Class model.

2. Mathematical Formulation: 4th-Tensor Equivalence Classes

In modern carrier stowage solvers (Hierarchical Decomposition: Master Bay Planning [MBPP] → Slot Planning [SPP]), the solver does not assign individual container serial numbers during hydrostatic optimization. It assigns interchangeable Equivalence Classes:

Legacy 3-Tensor Class:   Clegacy = ( p, l, w )  →  4 × 6 × 5 = 120 Classes
p ∈ Ports (4)  |  l ∈ Length/Type (6)  |  w ∈ VGM Weight Brackets (5)

Any two containers in the origin yard belonging to the same (p, l, w) class are 100% interchangeable in the vessel's stability and stress equations. ZeST appends a single discrete index—the Inland TEN-T Destination Zone (z ∈ Z, |Z| = 6) covering ZONE_A_DUI through ZONE_F_BUD:

ZeST 4-Tensor Class:   CZeST = ( p, l, w, z )  →  120 × 6 = 720 Classes
Solves to optimality in < 45 seconds per port call via standard MILP / CP-SAT

3. Proof of Hydrostatic Invariance (24,000-TEU ULCV Benchmark)

Consider a 24,000-TEU vessel arriving at Rotterdam Maasvlakte II with a 1,500-container (2,792 TEU) rail-bound cohort (mean VGM = 16.5 t, total vessel displacement Δ = 235,000 t). Rather than placing all ZONE_A_DUI boxes in one bay, the 4th-Tensor CSPP partitions each STS Crane Hatch Split (45 containers = 1 European 740m Block Train) into vertical weight tiers matching the vessel's exact hydrostatic profile:

Upper Deck Tiers (88–94) w1, w2 (Light / Med-Light) 2.0 – 14.0 t ZONE_A_DUI Preserves high-GM stability; discharged first onto outer wagon pins
Lower Deck / Upper Hold w3 (Medium) 14.0 – 20.0 t ZONE_A_DUI Neutral center-of-gravity tier; forms core 740m block consist
Lower Hold Tiers (02–08) w4, w5 (Heavy / X-Heavy) 20.0 – 32.5 t ZONE_A_DUI Lowers vertical center of gravity (KG); balances railcar axle loads

Within each 6.0 t VGM bracket (uniform intra-class standard deviation σw = 6.0 / √12 = 1.73 t), swapping containers of the same (p, l, w) across zones z yields a 1-sigma mass variance per 45-container train block of σblock = 1.732 × √45 = 11.62 tonnes. Across the 235,000-tonne displacement (Δ) and a mean vertical tier arm of ΔZ = 8.0 m, the maximum induced shift in Metacentric Height (ΔGM) is:

ΔGMmax = Ncohort · (σw / √45) · ΔZ Δvessel = 1,500 × 0.2582 t × 8.0 m 235,000 t = ± 0.0132 m (± 1.32 cm)
< 1% of the 1.50 m GM safety margin (below ballast sensor noise)

4. Why Origin Ports & Carriers Win: "Class-Level Grounding" (Zero Extra Shifters)

  • T-15d to T-6d (Origin Yard Grounding by Class): Export containers arriving at the Port of Origin (e.g., Shanghai, Ningbo, Singapore) are grounded in RTG/ASC stacks indexed by class (p, l, w, z) rather than individual serial number.
  • T-6d (Vessel Loading at Origin): Origin quay cranes pull any container from the matching (p, l, w, z) stack. If Container MAEU-111 is delayed at the origin gate, Container MAEU-222 from the same class takes its slot with zero origin yard re-handles.
  • T-5d / 120h at Sea (ZeST Crystallization Gate): Once at sea, the physical bay plan is locked and the ZeST Control Plane binds each container's EU 2020/1056 eFTI token + UCC NCTS T1 customs clearance to its exact 740m Block Train Wagon Pin at the European port.
