# European Seaport-to-TEN-T Intermodal Network

Interactive overlay of Europe's 17 major container gateways, 18 TEN-T seaport-to-dry-port rail spines, inland dry-port hubs, and toggleable OTR drayage radii (**150 km** single-day round-trip vs. **300 km** rail breakeven), plus **11 Voronoi catchment zones**, **legacy North-Range rail overreach**, and **14 high-friction rail border crossings**.

Use the layer panel in the top-right corner of the map to toggle overlays.
